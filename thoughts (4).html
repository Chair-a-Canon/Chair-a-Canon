Cognitive Sovereignty: The Choice to Remain Human in the Age of AI

We are not observers of the AI transformation — we are embedded participants, whether we acknowledge it or not. This manifesto argues against the false binary of AI-as-savior versus AI-as-destroyer, and for something more demanding: conscious cognitive collaboration that preserves human agency while leveraging artificial intelligence as an amplifier rather than a replacement. The choice is not whether to engage with AI. The choice is whether you do so with your eyes open.

We Shaped the World That Shaped Them: Understanding Gen Z Through Our Collective Journey

Gen Z's apparent nihilism is not pathology — it is philosophy. This paper traces a chain of unintended consequences from Nixon's 1971 gold standard decision through the social movements, economic shocks, and institutional collapses that followed, arguing that Gen Z's rejection of inherited meaning systems is exactly the response Nietzsche predicted: a necessary crisis preceding the construction of new values. We built the ladder to the American Dream. The ground beneath it shifted. That is not Gen Z's failure — it is our inheritance to them.

Beyond Good Intentions: A Male Leader's Playbook for Inclusive Cybersecurity Teams

Exclusion in cybersecurity teams is not merely a fairness issue — it is a critical security vulnerability that creates exploitable blind spots in defensive posture. Drawing on over 40 years of combined experience, this piece examines how unconscious bias undermines both team effectiveness and security outcomes, and provides male leaders with specific, actionable strategies for moving beyond good intentions toward measurably inclusive teams. Homogeneous teams think alike. Sophisticated adversaries count on it.
