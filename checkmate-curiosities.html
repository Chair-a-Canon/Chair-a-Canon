The Collections

Three worlds. Forty-eight pieces. No repeats.

Each collection is a complete set of 16 hand-painted originals — king, queen, bishops, knights, rooks, and pawns — unified by a theme and separated by the fact that no two pieces across any collection were ever painted the same way twice. View the full gallery to see every piece in detail.

Provenance & Authenticity

Every Checkmate Curiosities set begins as a blank 3D-printed PLA form — raw material, full of potential. What it becomes is decided once, by one hand, and never repeated. Each completed set ships with a certificate of authenticity confirming its singular origin under the Cognitive Sovereignty Studios maker's mark.
