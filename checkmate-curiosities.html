In the world of Checkmate Curiosities, the board is not a site of conflict, but a canvas of possibility. Each set is a curated collection of hand-painted characters and mineral-based forms—ranging from Steampunk Gnomes to tactical "Natasha Quackoff" ducks.

One-of-a-Kind: Every set is a singular original. We never repeat a theme, a palette, or a character. When you own a Checkmate Curiosities board, you own the only one in existence.
